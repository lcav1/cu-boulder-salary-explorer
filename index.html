READ THE NUMBERS WITH CONTEXT

What this data can—and can’t—tell you.

A yearly snapshot

The CU database is updated annually and reflects a single point in time during the fall term. Monthly checks on this dashboard do not mean the source changes monthly.

Annual base salary

Total salary includes all funds making up annual base salary. It excludes allowances, bonuses, and contractual additions. Values are shown as reported, without adjustment to 100% full-time.

Appointments, not people

The source states it excludes temporary workers, student employees, and retirees. Some published job titles still contain “temporary”; this dashboard preserves every Boulder record as published.

Employees with multiple appointments may appear more than once. Roster IDs identify employees where available; an employee-record identifier distinguishes their appointments.

How comparisons work

Dashboard filters affect every chart, summary, table row, and entered-salary comparison. Row peer groups always use the entire Boulder snapshot, including the current record.

Percentile is the percentage of salary records at or below a salary (ties included). Missing salaries are excluded from salary statistics. Median and percentiles use linear interpolation; full-time means at least 100%.

Range position = (salary − minimum) ÷ (maximum − minimum). It can be below 0% or above 100%; it is undefined if every salary is equal. Peer group sizes count records with known salaries.
